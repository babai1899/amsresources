| ABOUT US

We take great pleasure in introducing AMS RESOURCES, one of India’s leading and most reputed Human Resources consulting and recruitment service organizations. At AMS RESOURCES, we firmly believe that people are the most valuable assets of any organization. With this vision, AMS RESOURCES was established to deliver comprehensive manpower solutions that enhance organizational efficiency and strengthen talent pools to meet evolving business needs.

Our company has proven expertise in sourcing and deploying qualified professionals for specialized placements across diverse sectors, including Hospitals and Healthcare, Information Technology, Civil, Electrical, Mechanical Engineering, Marine Services, and Airport Operations.

We operate through associate offices across Gujarat, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Andhra Pradesh (Vizag), Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata, Odisha, and other major regions in India.

| OUR SERVICES

At the scale at which we operate, specialization is the cornerstone of recruitment efficiency. Given the diversity of industries and segments we serve, we have built a strong network of domain specialists who bring deep industry insight and practical expertise.

We identify and place top-tier professionals across industries, disciplines, and geographies worldwide. In many cases, seasoned professionals who have successfully completed distinguished careers are engaged to lead relevant practice divisions, allowing us to leverage their experience to identify and recruit talent of similar caliber.

This approach gives us a distinct advantage in specialized and senior-level recruitment, particularly for high-end and leadership positions. Additionally, our model fosters stronger relationships between our consultants, clients, and candidates, resulting in improved client engagement and consistently higher-quality recruitment outcomes.
